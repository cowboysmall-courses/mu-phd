Papers in the molecular computing metrics plots
==============================================

This list is the experimental corpus in analysis/paper_metrics.csv — the
points in the PDFs in this folder. Entries are grouped as in the plot 03
legend, then by year. The short name in brackets is the plot label
(paper_id).

Where a paper is forthcoming, the journal of record is given first and
the preprint is listed as well.
